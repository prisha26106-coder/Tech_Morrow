History and current status

As computers and electronics became more common, devices also began to be replaced more often. Smaller designs, rapid changes in technology and growing access to electronics have made e-waste a worldwide issue. Today, countries are working to improve repair, collection and responsible recycling systems.

Important global figures

  • 62 million tonnes: e-waste generated worldwide in 2022.
  • 22.3%: formally collected and recycled in documented systems in 2022.
  • 82 million tonnes: projected annual e-waste by 2030 if current trends continue.

These figures come from the Global E-waste Monitor 2024. For India-specific rules and updates, check the Central Pollution Control Board.
