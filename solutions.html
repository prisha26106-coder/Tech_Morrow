Solutions for your device

Choose a device from the list. The advice below will change to match your selection.

Smartphone

Donate or refurbish

If it works, securely erase your data and ask a trusted reuse group or refurbisher whether they accept it.

Reuse

A working phone can become a music player, offline camera or emergency backup, if the battery is safe.

Recycle

If it cannot be repaired or reused, take it to an appropriate e-waste collection point.

Before giving away a device, back up important files, sign out of accounts and follow the maker's instructions to erase personal data.
