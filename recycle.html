How to recycle e-waste

Recycling works best when devices are kept separate from everyday rubbish and handed to a suitable collection service. Different products may need different handling, especially batteries and damaged devices.

  1. Sort items: keep electronics, cables and batteries separate where possible.
  2. Back up and erase data: sign out and follow the device maker's secure reset instructions.
  3. Check the collection rules: ask what items the collector accepts.
  4. Use an authorized route: check government guidance or a recognized recycler.
  5. Keep proof: when available, ask for a receipt or collection confirmation.

India resource: CPCB E-Waste Management System. Confirm current collection arrangements before travelling.
