Environmental impact

When electronics are dumped, burned or dismantled carelessly, harmful substances can enter soil, water and air. Informal recycling can also put workers and nearby residents at risk. Responsible collection and treatment help recover useful materials and reduce pollution.

Land and soil

Unsafe dumping can release metals and other pollutants into soil and dust.

Water

Pollutants may reach drains, rivers or groundwater when waste is handled poorly.

Air

Burning wires and electronic parts can release harmful smoke and fumes.

People and wildlife

Exposure can affect workers, families and local ecosystems, especially where waste is processed without protection.

Case study: informal e-waste processing

Reports about informal e-waste processing areas, including Agbogbloshie in Ghana, have highlighted concerns about open burning, unsafe dismantling and pollution. The lesson is to support safe working conditions and proper recycling rather than informal burning.
