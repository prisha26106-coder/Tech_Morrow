Why is e-waste increasing?

E-waste grows when people replace devices quickly and when repair or collection is difficult. A device that is no longer useful to one person may still be repairable or useful to someone else.

  1. Fast technology changes: newer models encourage upgrades.
  2. Short useful life: some devices are difficult or expensive to repair.
  3. Consumer habits: people may replace items instead of repairing them.
  4. Limited repair options: spare parts or trusted repair services may be hard to find.
  5. Low collection awareness: old electronics are often stored at home or discarded incorrectly.
  6. More connected devices: households use more electronics than before.

We can help by choosing durable products, repairing when practical, buying refurbished devices and finding responsible collection routes.
