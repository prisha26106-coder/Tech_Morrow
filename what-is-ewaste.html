What is e-waste?

Electronic waste, or e-waste, means electrical or electronic equipment that someone has discarded, no longer wants, or cannot use. It includes whole devices and parts such as cables, chargers and circuit boards. Some items contain valuable materials, while others may contain substances that need careful handling.

Common examples

  • Phones, tablets and laptops
  • Televisions, monitors and printers
  • Chargers, cables and headphones
  • Small kitchen appliances
  • Batteries and power banks, which may need separate collection

Remember: an item is not automatically safe for the household rubbish bin just because it is small.
